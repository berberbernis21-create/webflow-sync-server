Scheduling & Storage

Pickup, delivery, or freight must be coordinated within 72 hours of purchase. We do not hold items — the best way to secure an item is to buy it.

Items not picked up or scheduled within 72 hours may be subject to storage fees unless prior arrangements are made with the store.

Storage fee $10.00 per day
Maximum storage 14 days

Orders placed after hours, on weekends, or on holidays may need extra response time.

Option 1: Self Pickup
Option 1

Self Pickup

Select Pick Up if you will collect the item at our Scottsdale showroom yourself, or if you already have your own delivery company arranging pickup.

  • We email next steps after purchase.
  • Self pickup does not include wrapping, palletizing, or transport prep.
  • Bring the right vehicle, help, blankets, and straps for large or awkward pieces.
  • If you do not have your own delivery company and need us to arrange local delivery, use Option 2 instead.
Option 2: Local Arizona Delivery
Option 2

Local Delivery

If you do not already have your own delivery company: select Pick Up at checkout, then fill out and submit the Delivery, Pickup & Freight calculator with your address, item details, and access information.

  • Filling out and submitting the form gives you a preliminary estimate and notifies our team to confirm final rates and help schedule.
  • You can get a quote first, then purchase — or purchase with Pick Up and submit the estimate right after.
  • After you submit, you can also reach out directly using the details from your estimate if you want to confirm sooner.
  • Typical rate: $95/hour (two movers + box truck).
  • Stairs, access, item size, extra crew, or longer trips can change the final amount.
  • Delivery charges are paid to the delivery company — not included in the item price.
Please fill out and submit the calculator — that is the fastest way for us to confirm pricing if you need delivery arranged. Questions? 480-588-7006 · info@lostandfoundresale.com
Option 3: Freight Preparation & Nationwide
Option 3

Freight Preparation

When standard shipping is not auto-calculated, select Freight Preparation at checkout, then fill out the Delivery, Pickup & Freight calculator for a preliminary freight range. We use that information to confirm a final rate with our freight partners.

  • Preparation fee: $10.00
  • Includes prep/palletizing when needed, staging, condition photos, and staff coordination for the pickup window.
  • Does not include carrier booking, freight charges, insurance, or delivery charges.
  • Freight pickups need at least 48 hours notice so we can stage the piece.
What the calculator helps with
  • Pallet-ready dimensions and weight for quoting.
  • A preliminary nationwide price range (not a locked carrier booking).
  • Access details (liftgate, residential, stairs, White Glove, and more).
Final freight rates are confirmed with freight partners after review. Estimates can move with carrier capacity, confirmed dims/weight, and access.
Customer responsibility

Customers are responsible for confirmed freight/delivery charges and carrier terms once a provider is booked.

  • Shipping, delivery, freight, and insurance costs.
  • Accessorials (missed pickup, redelivery, residential, storage, return, etc.).
  • Damage, loss, or delay after the carrier takes possession.
