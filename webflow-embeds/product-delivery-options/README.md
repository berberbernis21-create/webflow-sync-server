# Product page — Delivery, Pickup & Freight Options

Paste `PRODUCT-PAGE-delivery-options.html` into a **Webflow Embed** on the Product template (near shipping / below add to cart).

**Do not change** the product JSON-LD Before `</body>` block.

## What this copy does

- Standard shipping when checkout auto-calculates it
- Large/bulky without auto shipping → **Freight Preparation** + online estimator for a preliminary quote
- Local AZ → select local delivery at checkout, use the estimator (notifies team to confirm) or reach out directly after entering details
- Clear **we do not hold items** for unpaid quotes
- Scheduling: 72 hours / $10 day / 14 days
- Product-page local option does **not** name Cruz Pro Line yet
