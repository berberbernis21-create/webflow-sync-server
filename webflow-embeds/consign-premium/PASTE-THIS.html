What to include in your submission
  • Item name, category, brand or maker if known
  • Approximate age and original retail price if available
  • Clear photos of the front, side, back, labels, materials, and visible wear
  • Condition notes including scratches, stains, chips, repairs, odors, fabric wear, pet exposure, or missing pieces
  • Measurements including width, depth, and height
What is typically a strong fit
  • Clean, well cared for furniture, decor, art, lighting, rugs, mirrors, and accessories
  • Recognized brand, designer, maker, or strong style value when available
  • Good, very good, or excellent condition
  • Pieces with strong resale appeal and presentation value
  • Items that photograph well and fit our current showroom direction
What is usually not a fit
  • Heavy odors, pet damage, smoke exposure, major stains, or severe wear
  • Broken, unstable, incomplete, or structurally damaged items
  • Items requiring major cleaning, repair, refinishing, or restoration
  • Items with limited resale demand or poor presentation quality
  • Incomplete submissions with unclear photos or missing information
Consignment terms at a glance
  • Approved items are placed under a standard 90-day consignment agreement.
  • The standard consignment split is 50/50 of the final selling price.
  • Most accepted items are typically priced at approximately 30-50% of original retail.
  • Final pricing is determined after approval, intake, and in-person review, then provided through the consignment contract sent to you.
  • Final pricing is based on condition, brand, demand, style, presentation quality, current inventory needs, and market conditions.
  • The submission form allows up to 10 items and up to 30 photos total per submission.
  • Submitting an item does not guarantee acceptance.
After you submit

Our team reviews your submission and follows up by email if your items appear to be a good fit. If accepted, we provide next steps and approved delivery window options.

Accepted items must be delivered by the consignor or by a delivery provider arranged by the consignor.

Lost + Found Resale Interiors is selective by design. That selectivity helps protect the quality of the showroom, supports realistic pricing, and creates a stronger resale environment for approved pieces.
